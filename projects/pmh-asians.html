The role of mental illness in fatal police shootings

Zooming in on AAPI populations

In the last decade, just over 1 in 5 victims of fatal police shootings in the US involved people showing signs of mental illness. For Asian Americans and Pacific Islander populations, this rate is even higher. From 2015-2025, 29% of AAPI victims involved in fatal police shootings were showing signs of mental illness.
